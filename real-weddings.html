Rawla Shargun, Kumbhalgarh

Ritesh & Bhavika

Rawla Shargun, Kumbhalgarh

Bhavika and Ritesh wanted their wedding decor to be a perfect blend of royal elegance and personal touches. They aimed for a luxurious, rich, and detailed look that still felt warm and inviting. Each event was to have a unique style fitting its mood – from pastel freshness at Haldi to glamorous and fairy-tale-like settings for the Sangeet, with a grand aesthetic backdrop for the wedding itself.

Ritesh & Bhavika

The Oberoi Udaivilas, Udaipur

Aarav & Ananya

The Oberoi Udaivilas, Lake Pichola

Ananya and Aarav envisioned a Lake Pichola royal celebration immersed in candlelight, floating lotus mandaps, and regal Mewari heritage. They wanted every guest arriving by royal boat to feel the magic of Rajasthan. SHOWMANIA designed an ethereal Sangeet with crystal canopies and a Pheras ceremony illuminated by 10,000 diyas reflecting on the lake.

Aarav & Ananya

Fairmont Jaipur, Kukas

Karan & Meera

Fairmont Palace, Jaipur

Meera and Karan requested a grand Pink City extravaganza with high-energy Punjabi and Marwari fusion. They wanted a carnival-themed Mehndi with live folk percussionists, an electric Mughal-glam Sangeet featuring celebrity singers, and a sunset courtyard wedding with cascading tuberose and pink roses.

Karan & Meera

W Goa & Vagator Beachfront

Rohan & Tanya

Vagator Cliff & Beach, Goa

Tanya and Rohan wanted a barefoot coastal chic vibe mixed with high-octane sunset cocktail parties. The Haldi by the pool featured marigold yellow and turquoise accents, while the beachfront Pheras took place under a bamboo canopy at golden hour with sound waves harmonizing with acoustic sitar rhythms.
